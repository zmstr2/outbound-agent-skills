# Persona library

Generic buying roles that apply to any play. A play's "Personas in this play" section adds what each cares about for that play, and may define custom personas or override these. Match contacts by what they own, not just by title.

## Economic buyer
**Typical titles:** C-level or VP who owns the budget for the function (CFO, CHRO, CMO, CRO, COO, VP of the function).
**Role in the deal:** signs off. Answers upward for cost, risk and results.
**Measured on:** hitting targets, cost, credibility with the top team, time for strategy rather than firefighting.
**How to pitch:** money, risk and credibility. Lead with the business consequence. Never tool detail or day-to-day admin.
**Email them?** Yes.

## Champion
**Typical titles:** head of or director of the function that owns the problem day to day (operations, enablement, transformation, programme or product lead).
**Role in the deal:** carries the case internally; often career-minded and willing to back a new approach.
**Measured on:** landing their programme, credibility with the economic buyer, time for strategy.
**How to pitch:** the pain of doing the work and the credibility it costs them. Practical and specific; not board-level targets.
**Email them?** Yes; often the best first contact.

## User
**Typical titles:** analyst, specialist, manager or coordinator who does the hands-on work the product changes.
**Role in the deal:** doesn't buy; their pain is evidence for the champion and buyer.
**Measured on:** getting the work right, on time, through every revision.
**How to pitch:** rarely email them. Use their pain (job ads, posts, their team's hiring) as evidence in emails to the champion. If asked, keep it about their own time and workload.
**Email them?** Rarely.

## Technical evaluator
**Typical titles:** CTO, CIO, head of engineering, IT or security lead, architect.
**Role in the deal:** checks fit, security and integration; can push for a change or block one.
**Measured on:** delivery speed, reliability, security, operating cost, leaders' time lost to tooling.
**How to pitch:** in delivery and risk terms, not business-user terms. Never lead with features.
**Email them?** Yes where the product touches their systems or team.

## Blocker or gatekeeper
**Typical titles:** procurement, legal, compliance, finance business partner, an incumbent-tool owner.
**Role in the deal:** slows or stops deals; may own the current approach.
**How to pitch:** don't cold-email to sell. Make sure the champion has what they need to answer this person's questions.
**Email them?** No, unless the play says otherwise.

## Custom personas
If none fits, copy this block into the play file under "Personas in this play":

```markdown
- **<Persona> (<titles>).** Role in the deal: <…>. Email them? <yes / rarely / no>. Measured on: <…>. How to pitch: <…>. Pains in this play: <…>.
```
